# Problem selection: Saudi Vision 2030

The Vision 2030 pillars are Vibrant Society, Thriving Economy, and Ambitious Nation. The Health Sector Transformation and Quality of Life programs provide relevant policy context for road-safety analysis; this demonstration does **not** claim an official program KPI.

| Candidate | Vision fit | SDG impact | Open-data readiness | One-repo feasibility | Measurability | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Road-safety incident analysis | 5 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 5 | **23** |
| Food waste during pilgrim seasons | 4 | 5 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 20 |
| Water scarcity/desalination | 5 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 20 |
| Urban heat and energy | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 19 |
| Primary-care appointment triage | 5 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 18 |

Scores are **project-team judgments**, not official ratings. Open-data readiness is provisional; synthetic records are used until a suitable licensed dataset is validated.

**Selected:** Road-safety incident analysis. **Users:** road-safety analysts, municipal planners, transport researchers. **Pillar:** Vibrant Society. **Program relevance:** Quality of Life / Health Sector Transformation (contextual, not an asserted formal KPI). **Proposed project KPI:** number of incidents reviewed and validated high-risk sites flagged for human audit.

**Problem statement:** Road-safety planners need interpretable ways to summarize incident patterns and prioritize potential safety audits. This agent ingests privacy-preserving incident records, identifies concentration by area and hour, creates a simple baseline, and produces bilingual, SDG-tagged recommendations. Outputs are decision support only; synthetic demonstrations must not be used as evidence of real accident hotspots.
