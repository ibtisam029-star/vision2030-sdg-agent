# SDG alignment and safeguards

**Primary:** SDG **3.6**, reduce deaths and injuries from road traffic accidents; **indicator 3.6.1**: death rate due to road traffic injuries. The sample cannot estimate a population-based death rate.

**Secondary:** SDG **11.2**, safe, affordable, accessible and sustainable transport; **indicator 11.2.1**: proportion of population with convenient access to public transport, by sex, age and persons with disabilities. Our pedestrian-safety audit suggestion is related to the target, but **does not measure** indicator 11.2.1.

| Agent capability | Vision 2030 relevance | SDG target | Local measurable metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incident aggregation | Vibrant Society / safer mobility | 3.6 | incident and injury counts |
| District concentration detection | Quality of Life (contextual) | 3.6 | incidents per district |
| Human-reviewed pedestrian audit suggestions | Quality of Life (contextual) | 11.2 | number of proposed audits reviewed |
| Baseline forecasting | Health Sector Transformation (contextual) | 3.6 | held-out forecast error, once longitudinal data exists |

**Risks:** Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) compliance requires legal review, minimization, access controls and retention limits before handling personal data. Exclude names, plates, exact home locations and identifiers. Evaluate representation across regions and language groups; validate Arabic translations with domain specialists. Sparse data can stigmatize areas; avoid presenting incident counts as exposure-adjusted risk. All decisions remain human-in-the-loop. The agent must **never** independently assign legal fault, identify drivers, issue fines, direct police deployments, or authorize road closures.

Sources: https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal3 ; https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal11 ; https://unstats.un.org/sdgs/metadata/ ; https://sdaia.gov.sa/
